Prepared 2026-10-17 under D-042 A. This is an inventory and proposed operating procedure; it activates no production schedule and changes no retention period.

## Proposed production operating procedure

Product owner is primary incident/support contact; Rohin is backup. Before launch, record a private operational contact channel, coverage hours, handover timing and an authorized operator for maintenance failures. A nomination does not provision application or cloud access.

The deployment owner configures private worker authentication in the approved secret store, with exact target/environment checks and attributable changes. Keep credentials out of Cron SQL, URLs, source, logs and screenshots. Run each bounded job without overlapping destructive batches; retry only its unfinished work and record attempted/completed counts. Never widen eligibility or extend retention automatically to make a failed run look successful.

Observe both scheduler execution and work completion. Treat exhausted retry, stale success, unexpected backlog growth, object-removal failure and metadata-finalization mismatch as distinct failures. A 200 response with `purged < examined` requires inspection; it is not full cleanup success. Do not print filenames, object paths, student identifiers or raw provider errors in general alerts. Keep necessary detail restricted to authorized investigation.

On failure, preserve references and holds, stop the affected destructive job if its scope or state is uncertain, record minimized evidence and contact the accountable operator. Independent notification/read operations may continue if safe. Re-enable only after an exact-scope replay with expected references/holds intact. Do not use a replacement user's session as compensation. Unknown objects and ambiguous password mutations remain protected for investigation.

## Required rehearsal before production activation

Exercise missed scheduling, a delayed worker, overlapping invocations, unavailable Storage, deletion success with lost completion response, repeated execution and recovery after interruption. Verify eligible bytes are absent through Storage and protected referenced/held files still download. Check that cleanup progress and failure counts reflect the actual outcome. Rehearse external observation while the app/backend is unavailable. Use synthetic records and exact fixture cleanup until production is explicitly approved.

The current F022/F023/F029/F030 tests provide parts of this evidence; they do not establish a complete production maintenance service. Frequency, catch-up budget, private diagnostic retention, alert channel and activation remain unresolved operational choices. Keep the release checklist unchecked until those are approved and tested.
