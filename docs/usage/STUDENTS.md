# Student guide

Assignments may include named reference files supplied by your teacher. Read the instructions, then use **Assignment reference files** to download each relevant reference. Names explain the file's purpose; these are separate from your own submission uploads. Downloads require current assignment access.

[Handbook home](README.md) · [Access and help](ACCESS_AND_TROUBLESHOOTING.md)

## 1. Activate and sign in

New students receive a Dexam Member ID and a one-time activation pack privately from an authorized enrolment operator. Open its activation link (`/activate`), follow the identity/proof prompts and choose your own password. Use the pack's backup entry if the link cannot be opened. Keep the pack private; do not send it in a group or screenshot. Used/expired credentials cannot be replayed—contact the operator rather than creating another account.

Open `/sign-in` and enter your Member ID and password. Member ID is your stable Dexam identity; joining another course or finishing a course does not require a duplicate account. The student login is not an employee email login.

If you forget your password, use `/student/recover` for the assisted-recovery instructions or to consume a privately issued recovery pack. Two eligible administrators must independently authorize a reset after checking established identity/enrolment evidence. A name or Member ID alone is not enough. Hosted reset execution remains disabled pending launch approval; this is not public email self-service.

## 2. Home and courses

Open `/student`. Review your Member ID, current enrolments, offering/cohort details and current access status. An account without an active, assigned course will not automatically have lessons. Access is rechecked; old links do not override a suspended account or ended enrolment.

## 3. Read coursework

Open **Coursework** (`/student/coursework`). Choose the available course/curriculum, browse sections and topics, and read the published lessons. Open/download available private lesson files through the provided controls. Only published, assigned material is available; staff drafts are not visible.

The course is tied to its assigned published version. A staff member publishing a newer version does not silently replace your existing curriculum. Lesson files are for currently active assigned access, not part of the post-course work archive. If no material appears, ask staff to check publication and course assignment.

## 4. Submit an assignment

1. Open **Assignments** (`/student/assignments`) and select an assignment actually released to you.
2. Read its brief, instructions, reference materials and due-date information before starting. An optional due date does not by itself prove late submissions are blocked; follow the displayed assignment policy.
3. Choose **Start submission**. This creates a draft attempt.
4. Select **Add PDF or images**. Accepted types are PDF, JPEG, PNG and WebP; the displayed limits are 10 files, 10 MB each and 50 MB total per attempt.
5. Review the prepared files. Eligible images are optimized/privacy-cleaned on your device. Inspect detail carefully for drawing work; choose **Keep higher quality** when needed. PDFs are not promised equivalent image compression. You can remove prepared files before uploading.
6. Choose **Upload reviewed files**. Wait until files appear under **Uploaded privately**. In the draft you can remove uploaded files and correct the set.
7. Once every intended file is uploaded and no prepared files remain, choose **Submit assignment**. Uploading alone is not submission. The attempt becomes available for teacher review; it remains editable until feedback is published.
8. Confirm the status/attempt history changed. If the response is uncertain, refresh and check history before trying again; do not repeatedly start duplicate work.

Before published feedback, choose **Edit attempt 1** (or the relevant number) to reopen that attempt as a draft. Existing files are carried forward; add/remove files and deliberately submit again. The displayed attempt number stays the same and its version increases. The prior submitted snapshot and any private teacher draft are preserved internally. While editing, the new version is not available for review; stale feedback cannot be published against the old version.

Once feedback is published, that reviewed attempt stays locked, including a correction-request outcome. Use **Start another attempt** to create Attempt 2, Attempt 3 and so on. You do not need a correction request, but current course access and an active assignment release are required. Only one draft per assignment is allowed; finish it before editing another attempt. Existing file limits remain, and repeated attempts do not promise unlimited storage.

## 5. Read feedback

Open the assignment's **Attempt history** and published feedback. Read the teacher's final written feedback and correction instructions; play an optional published voice note where present. Private teacher drafts or unaccepted AI suggestions are not student feedback. AI-assisted-and-teacher-reviewed disclosure may be shown when relevant.

If the outcome is review completed, the teacher has finished that review—not awarded an exam score or certified mastery. If correction is requested, prepare the next attempt against that specific guidance. The current product does not supply an assessment/scoring system merely from these statuses.

## 6. Notifications

Open `/student/notifications`, review all/unread updates, open the relevant protected work item, and mark individual/all updates read as appropriate. Messages cover existing approved events such as released assignments, published feedback and class changes. Reading a notice does not submit work or change a grade. An old notice cannot restore ended access. These are in-app updates; email, SMS, WhatsApp, push and advance reminders are not automatically enabled.

## 7. Class agenda

Open **Class agenda** (`/student/agenda`) to see currently permitted one-off class sessions and schedule changes/cancellations. Check the displayed time and timezone. The app does not promise recurring classes, attendance tracking, conferencing links, or a reminder 24 hours before class. If no session is shown, verify your course/cohort with staff.

## 8. Private study checklist

Open `/student/study-checklist`. Mark/unmark topics while active to track your own study. This is self-reported, private progress—not teacher-verified achievement, attendance, marks or passive tracking. After course closeout, retained checklist information can be read within the existing archive window but not used to resume academic access.

## 9. After the course ends

Course completion/withdrawal ends lessons and new submissions for that enrolment. Your Member ID and other eligible courses remain separate. Open **Work archive** (`/student/archive`) to privately view/download eligible finalized submissions and already-published feedback during the 12-month window after course end. It is not lifetime storage, editable submission space, or an archive of all lessons/drafts.

Download any work you need to keep before access expires. The app may display expiry information; do not rely on an external reminder. Access expiry is separate from eventual physical retention cleanup and approved holds. Missing/expired items cannot be restored by guessing old file links.

## 10. Common problems

| What you see                       | What to do                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Invalid Member ID/password         | Check the exact Member ID and use assisted recovery; do not try the Zoho/employee password merely because it works elsewhere.               |
| No coursework or assignment        | Ask staff to check active enrolment, published curriculum assignment and deliberate assignment release.                                     |
| Upload rejected                    | Check format, file/total limits and whether the attempt is still a draft. Review image preparation rather than uploading unsupported types. |
| Uploaded but teacher cannot review | Ensure you selected **Submit assignment**, not only upload.                                                                                 |
| Access denied or course closed     | Contact staff about current access. A browser refresh or old URL cannot override it.                                                        |
| Wrong file submitted               | Use Edit attempt before published feedback. If reviewed, keep it locked and start another attempt while access remains active.              |

Never send your password, backup code, private activation/recovery link or other students' files to support.
